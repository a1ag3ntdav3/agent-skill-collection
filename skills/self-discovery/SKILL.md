# Self-Discovery

Use this skill when a task is unique, complex, or does not fit standard sequential reasoning workflows, requiring a customized cognitive strategy.

## Instructions
1. **Select**: Choose relevant reasoning strategies from the following library:
   - *Decomposition*: Break down into independent subproblems.
   - *First-Principles*: Strip to fundamental truths and build up.
   - *Sanity Check*: Verify outputs against basic reality rules.
   - *Perspective-Taking*: Analyze from a user/reviewer persona.
   - *Constraint Audit*: Track and enforce strict rules or resource limits.
2. **Adapt**: Rewrite the selected strategies to target your specific prompt.
3. **Structure**: Organize the adapted strategies into a step-by-step checklist.
4. **Execute**: Run through the customized checklist to produce the final output.
